DEPARTMENT OVERVIEW

ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE & INDUSTRIAL FOCUS

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering was established at the Yenepoya Institute of Technology (formerly Dr. M.V. Shetty Institute of Technology) in the year 2008 with an approved intake of 60 students. The department runs an undergraduate programme in Electronics and Communication Engineering.

Recognized by AICTE and affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) Belagavi, the department offers a broad-based UG curriculum with a unique mix of electrical, electronics, and computer-related courses enabling students to pursue professional careers and higher research worldwide.

YEN EMBIENCE 2026

ABOUT THE EVENT

An inter-college technical platform organized by Echolectic & the Department of ECE at Yenepoya Institute of Technology. Designed to bring together bright minds to compete, collaborate, and solve critical engineering challenges.

01

BUILD.

Interface microcontrollers, wire sensors, and construct robust physical circuits under time constraints.

02

TEST.

Debug firmware, calibrate signal feedback, and validate component operational tolerances.

03

SOLVE.

Transform live problem statements into functional embedded and mechanical solutions.

04

PRESENT.

Demonstrate working prototypes live before expert jury panels and peer engineers.

COMPETITION ARENA

THE CHALLENGE FLOOR

ELIGIBILITY & GUIDELINES

WHO CAN PARTICIPATE?

Strict compliance with competition guidelines ensures a fair and competitive engineering floor.

01
The event is open to undergraduate and diploma students from recognized colleges and universities.
02
Each Build-A-Thon team must consist of exactly 4 members. No partial teams permitted.
03
Cross-department teams are fully permitted to encourage multi-disciplinary engineering solutions.
04
Cross-year teams are allowed, enabling senior and junior student collaborations.
05
Participants must strictly follow individual event safety standards and registration requirements.
